# Code of Conduct

Reflow Studio is a community project. People share what they build, help each other run their instances and enter competitions with their generations. We want every one of those places to be welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The summary below is for quick reading; where it differs from the Covenant, the Covenant applies.

## In short

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to self-hosting or to AI tools.
- Comment on the work, not on the person. Disagree openly and politely, and accept a decision once it is made.
- No harassment, insults, trolling, sexualised language or imagery, and no personal or political attacks.
- Do not publish other people's private information (email addresses, keys, unpublished generations) without their explicit permission.
- Shared generations and competition entries follow the same rules. Never post content that sexualises minors, intimate or degrading imagery of real people without their consent, or material that harasses or demeans a person or group. Respect the terms of the provider that generated it.

## Where it applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, competitions and any community chat linked from this repository. It also applies when you represent the project somewhere else.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, tell the maintainers:

- by email: **conduct@example.com**, or
- privately on GitHub: open the **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and start the title with `Conduct:`. Only maintainers can read these reports.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers review every report and reply as soon as they can. A maintainer who is involved in a report does not take part in handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct. Consequences follow the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant: a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban or, for serious or repeated violations, a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
